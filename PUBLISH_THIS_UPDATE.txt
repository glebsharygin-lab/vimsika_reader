To publish lexical and syntax annotations for collaborators, also replace
collaboration-worker/src/index.js with the packaged version and redeploy the
Worker from the collaboration-worker folder:

npm run deploy

The Worker upgrade preserves existing editorial-overrides.json content and
adds lexiconEntries and syntaxAnnotations during the next publication.

DharmaNexus matches are not copied into the corpus. An authorized export can be
imported with scripts/build_trial_corpus.py --authorized-alignments FILE.json.
