# Slavic witnesses: source survey, 8 September 2026

The reader contains 45 witnesses. Five are Slavic: three previously supplied
Polish/Russian translations and two newly verified excerpts. An excerpt is
registered as a partial witness; missing verses are not reconstructed.

Petek's article identifies a complete translation of the verses in her book
[*Na pragu prebujenja: svetovi sanj v budizmu*](https://beletrina.si/knjiga/na-pragu-prebujenja)
(Ljubljana: Beletrina, 2022), pp. 196–198. The full translation was not available
in the accessible material and is **not** included in this release.

Searches for Czech, Slovak, Ukrainian, Serbian, Croatian and other Slavic
versions did not yield another verifiable, accessible scholarly text suitable
for ingestion in this pass. This is a search outcome, not a claim that such
translations do not exist. Czech encyclopedic title references were insufficient
to establish a translator, edition and text. Ukrainian material on Vasubandhu
encountered during the search concerned other works and was not treated as a
Viṃśikā translation. References to A. Orlov's Russian work remain bibliographic
leads requiring an edition-level text check.

The new excerpts are short attributed quotations. The complete books and
articles have not been republished. Existing supplied witness texts and their
rights labels are preserved. All new alignment selections remain drafts pending
scholarly review.
