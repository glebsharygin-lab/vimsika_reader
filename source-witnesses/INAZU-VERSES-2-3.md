# Reading Inazu’s second and third verses

The Japanese witness gains 19 draft phrase links drawn from 18 selections on
pages 354–356 (PDF pages 12–14). In Focus, expand **Alignment note** to see the
printed reading or the reason for the chosen boundaries.

In the city-of-the-gandharvas example on p. 355, the scan has 乾闥婆城. The OCR
drops 婆 and places small explanatory type before the end of the question,
非ざるか. The ending is fused with a gloss inside one saved token. Unit 2.11 links the
identifiable main run before the interruption. Unit 2.10 also has a fused
ending. Both cells carry a partial-coverage label. Their Focus notes give the
printed negative. In verse 3 an ellipsis separates two selected runs around
a stray OCR fragment at the page break in 3.5 and the small verse reference
within the common-sight statement in 3.10–11.

Inazu’s verse 3 begins 場所等の限定は成立す / 夢の如し。 The OCR misreads 夢 as 砂.
On p. 356 the scan has 相續の不限定なる。 The Japanese places 皆同じなる at the
end of the common-sight example, so 3.10 and 3.11 share a selection.
