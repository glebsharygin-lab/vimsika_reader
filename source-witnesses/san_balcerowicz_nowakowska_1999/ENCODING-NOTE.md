# Balcerowicz–Nowakowska Sanskrit Encoding Note

The Sanskrit pages use Piotr Balcerowicz’s custom **AMRITA** Devanāgarī
fonts. The PDF contains searchable character codes, but those codes do not map
to Unicode Devanāgarī or Unicode Roman transliteration. Consequently:

- `edition-legacy.txt` preserves the extracted source codes and page order.
- `edition-unicode-draft-devanagari.txt` is a first rule-based Unicode
  Devanāgarī draft produced from the embedded AmritaA encoding.
- `conversion-report.md` records unresolved glyph codes and remaining risks.
- The draft must not yet be used for tokenization, word alignment, collation,
  or display as a normal Sanskrit witness.
- A diplomatic Unicode transcription should be produced from the visible
  Devanāgarī pages and checked against the `B` readings recorded in the
  Ruzsa–Szegedi critical apparatus.

This limitation affects only the Sanskrit edition. The Polish translation has
been recovered separately from its PolishTimes encoding.
