# Inazu’s second verse

Kizō Inazu’s 1929 translation prints verse 2 in the four large-type columns at the right of printed page 354 (PDF page 12). Reading them from right to left gives this draft transcription. The brackets mark the small inline gloss.

> 若し表識にして無境的〔無對象的〕なりせば、  
> 場所と時との限定と  
> 相續の不限定と  
> 及び作用とは可能ならず。  

The Japanese places the condition first. Both Sanskrit units 2.1 and 2.2 therefore share the whole root. The OCR misreads 相續 as 和績 and damages the closing negative. The following large-type question 何の所意なりや。 corresponds to Sanskrit 2.3, kim uktaṃ bhavati. Its small explanatory gloss begins after the selected span.
