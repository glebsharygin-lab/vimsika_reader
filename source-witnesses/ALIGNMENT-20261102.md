Shared groups retain complete English sentences when the Sanskrit units divide

result. The link identifies a source passage; it does not certify clean OCR or
exact semantic agreement. In particular, the notes preserve these distinctions:

None of these source choices was rewritten. Existing OCR warnings remain, and
no speculative text or links were added for omissions or cross-passage material.
